What does Kiran Growth School do?+

Kiran Growth School is a digital and performance marketing agency working with all types of businesses across India. We run Meta Ads, Google Ads, social media management, website development (live in 48 hours), AI videos, AI avatar videos and AI automation. We have special expertise in solar companies and real estate, and we also run an academy with Meta Ads and AI courses.

Do you work only in Delhi NCR?+

No. We work pan India, with clients managed over WhatsApp, calls and shared dashboards. We have a strong local focus in Delhi, Delhi NCR (Gurugram, Ghaziabad, Noida, Faridabad) and Madhya Pradesh (Bhopal, Rewa, Satna).

Which types of businesses do you work with?+

All kinds: solar and real estate (our specialities), clinics and healthcare, coaching and education, D2C and e-commerce brands, restaurants and cafes, interior designers, manufacturers, B2B companies, startups and local service businesses.

Can you really make a website in 48 hours?+

Yes. A standard business website goes live within 48 hours once we have your logo, content, photos and contact details. Bigger projects get a clear timeline upfront.

Why do you specialise in solar and real estate?+

Both are high-ticket, high-trust purchases where buyers research online before they call, and where fast follow-up decides who wins the sale. Our ad, website and WhatsApp automation playbooks are built for exactly that.
