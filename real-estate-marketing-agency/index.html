Do real estate ads need a RERA number?+

Yes. State RERA authorities, including UP RERA, HRERA, Delhi RERA and MP RERA, expect registered projects to show their RERA registration number and website in advertisements, and several have issued specific advertising directions. We add it to every ad and landing page.

Are Meta Ads good for real estate in India?+

Yes, especially for new launches, plots and mid-segment housing. Lead forms with qualifying questions, click-to-WhatsApp ads and video walkthroughs work well when followed up within minutes.

Do you work with brokers and channel partners?+

Yes. Channel partners can run their own ads for the projects they are mandated on, as long as the creatives and claims are approved and RERA-compliant.

Can you get NRI buyers?+

We can target NRI audiences in the Gulf, UK, US and elsewhere on Meta, Google and YouTube for projects that suit them, with timezone-aware WhatsApp follow-up.
