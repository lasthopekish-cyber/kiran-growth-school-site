Bhopal is one of the better rooftop solar markets in Madhya Pradesh. Large parts of the city are independent houses with flat RCC roofs, summer bills are high, and the PM Surya Ghar subsidy has made homeowners curious. Yet many installers still say the same thing: "We get enquiries, but very few turn into site surveys." Getting good **solar leads in Bhopal** is less about more ads and more about answering the subsidy and net metering questions before the buyer asks.

This guide is for solar installers and EPC companies serving Bhopal. It covers what buyers need to understand, the campaigns that bring qualified enquiries, and a simple follow-up system. Scheme figures are as of November 2026; always confirm on official sources before quoting.

## What Bhopal buyers want to know first

Almost every homeowner who clicks a solar ad has the same four questions:

1. **How much subsidy will I get?** As of November 2026, the central subsidy under PM Surya Ghar is ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third kW. That is ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and a maximum of ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above.
2. **How much will I pay?** The system price minus subsidy, or a loan. Give a range and say final pricing depends on the roof survey.
3. **Is it safe, and who will do the paperwork?** Most buyers worry about the online application and DISCOM visits more than the panels.
4. **What happens to extra units?** This is where net metering needs a clear, simple explanation.

The scheme is real and moving fast. MNRE said in August 2026 that more than 50 lakh households had installed rooftop solar under PM Surya Ghar. Point buyers to [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in) so they can check details themselves; it builds trust instead of reducing it.

Avoid "free solar panels" or "zero bill guaranteed" in ads. Say "subsidy up to ₹78,000" and "savings depend on usage".

## Solar leads in Bhopal: explain the MPMKVVCL process in five steps

Bhopal is served by **MPMKVVCL**, the Madhya Kshetra (central zone) DISCOM. Put this simple version of the process on your landing page and in your WhatsApp replies:

| Step | What happens | Who does it |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Apply | Register on the PM Surya Ghar portal, select Madhya Pradesh and MPMKVVCL, enter the consumer (IVRS) number from the bill | Homeowner, with installer's help |
| 2. Feasibility | MPMKVVCL checks sanctioned load and transformer capacity | DISCOM |
| 3. Install | A vendor registered on the portal installs ALMM-listed panels and the inverter | Installer |
| 4. Inspect and net meter | Installer uploads documents, DISCOM inspects and fits the net meter, commissioning report goes on the portal | Installer and DISCOM |
| 5. Subsidy | Homeowner submits bank details and the subsidy is credited by DBT | Homeowner, via portal |

Timelines vary by DISCOM workload, season and how complete the documents are, so give a range rather than a promise. One practical tip: tell buyers early to keep their latest electricity bill, Aadhaar, a cancelled cheque and property proof ready. Flat owners will also need a society NOC.

On net metering, keep it to one sentence in ads: "Units you don't use go to the grid and are adjusted in your bill." Export rates and billing rules are set by the MP regulator and can change, so check the current MPMKVVCL and MPERC position before putting numbers in creatives. ## Three campaigns that bring real solar leads in Bhopal

### 1. Google Search for people already looking

These buyers are closest to a decision. Use tight ad groups:

- "solar panel price Bhopal", "3kW solar system price Bhopal"
- "rooftop solar subsidy Bhopal", "PM Surya Ghar Bhopal"
- "solar installer near me" (with a tight radius around your service area)
- Hindi variations such as "सोलर पैनल सब्सिडी भोपाल"

Add negatives for jobs, wholesale, "free" and courses. Our [Google Ads guide for solar installers](/blog/google-ads-for-solar-installers-keywords/) has a full keyword and negative list.

### 2. Meta Ads and reels to create demand

Most homeowners in Kolar Road, Awadhpuri, Arera Colony, Bawadiya Kalan or Narmadapuram Road have not searched yet. Meta reaches them first. What tends to work:

- A 30 to 45 second Hindi reel showing a real Bhopal rooftop installation (with the owner's permission)
- A simple subsidy explainer with the ₹78,000 figure and an "as of" date
- A lead form or WhatsApp click ad with two qualifying questions: average monthly bill and house type (independent or flat)

Our guide to [Meta Ads for solar companies](/blog/meta-ads-for-solar-companies-india/) covers the full setup.

### 3. Referrals from installed customers

A neighbour's rooftop is the strongest ad in any colony. After each installation, ask for a short video testimonial (only real customers, only with consent), a Google review, and a referral. A small, clearly stated referral thank-you is fine; avoid promises you cannot keep.

## Landing page checklist for Bhopal

Do not send solar leads to your home page. Build one page for Bhopal homeowners with:

- Headline with "rooftop solar in Bhopal" and the subsidy figure with an "as of" date
- The five-step MPMKVVCL process table above
- A simple savings estimator (labelled illustrative)
- Loan option: PM Surya Ghar offers collateral-free loans through public sector banks; PIB reported the rate as repo plus 0.5% in July 2026, so check the current rate before quoting
- Photos of your own Bhopal installations, MNRE/portal vendor registration and warranty details
- Click-to-call and WhatsApp buttons, and a short form

## Follow up in minutes, not hours

Most lost solar leads are lost in the first hour. A basic system:

1. Instant WhatsApp reply with the subsidy explainer and three questions (bill, roof type, sanctioned load)
2. Call within 15 minutes during working hours
3. Book the site survey on the call, then send a reminder the day before
4. Re-engage non-responders after 3 days and 10 days with a reel or customer video
