"How much will a website cost?" is the first question almost every business owner in Delhi, Noida, Gurugram and Ghaziabad asks us. The honest answer is that **website design cost in Delhi NCR** ranges from a few thousand rupees to several lakh, and the number on a quote tells you very little until you know what is inside it.

This guide breaks down typical 2026 prices by website type, the yearly running costs most quotes leave out, and a short checklist to compare two quotes fairly. All figures are typical market ranges as of November 2026. Your own price will depend on scope.

## Website design cost in Delhi NCR by type of website

| Website type | Typical range (one-time) | Usual timeline | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| One-page / landing page | ₹5,000 – ₹20,000 | 1–3 days | A single offer, ad campaigns, events |
| Standard business site (5–8 pages) | ₹10,000 – ₹60,000 | 2 days – 3 weeks | Shops, clinics, coaching centres, service firms |
| Industry site with lead tools (calculator, booking, WhatsApp flows) | ₹30,000 – ₹1,20,000 | 2–5 weeks | Solar installers, real estate, education |
| E-commerce (up to ~100 products) | ₹30,000 – ₹1,50,000 | 3–6 weeks | Bakeries, retail, D2C brands |
| Custom web app or portal | ₹1,00,000 and above | 2–6 months | Booking systems, dashboards, member portals |

Two businesses can ask for "a 5-page website" and get quotes of ₹12,000 and ₹55,000, and both can be fair. The difference is usually in four places:

1. **Design approach.** A ready theme with your colours is quicker and cheaper than a layout designed from scratch.
2. **Content.** If you supply the text and photos, the price drops. If the agency writes service pages in English and Hindi, it rises, and it should.
3. **SEO setup.** Page titles, meta descriptions, schema, Google Business Profile linking, sitemap and Search Console are real work. Cheap quotes often skip them.
4. **Lead features.** WhatsApp click tracking, enquiry forms that go to your phone, Meta Pixel and Google tag setup turn a website into a lead source.

## The yearly costs most quotes don't mention

A website is not a one-time bill. These are the running costs to budget for every year:

| Item | Typical yearly cost (as of Nov 2026) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Domain (.in or .com) | ₹500 – ₹1,500 | Renewal prices are often higher than the first-year offer |
| Hosting | ₹2,000 – ₹10,000 | Shared hosting is fine for most small business sites |
| SSL certificate | Usually included | The free certificate bundled with most hosting plans is enough for most small business sites |
| Business email | A few hundred rupees per user per month | Optional, but looks more professional than a Gmail ID |
| Updates and maintenance | ₹0 – ₹2,000+ a month | Depends on how often you change prices, offers or photos |

Also check one thing before you sign: **the domain should be registered in your name**, with your email as the owner. It is a common problem for business owners in Delhi NCR to find they cannot move their website because a past developer holds the domain login.

## What changes the price up or down

**Things that lower cost:**

- You send the logo, text, photos and contact details in one go
- You choose a proven layout rather than a fully custom design
- You start with fewer pages and add more later

**Things that raise cost:**

- Bilingual pages (Hindi and English) written from scratch
- Online payments, product catalogues or booking systems
- Many location pages (for example separate pages for Noida, Gurugram and Ghaziabad)
- Custom illustrations, video shoots or professional photography

For most local businesses, the smartest path is a fast, clean site now and steady additions later. A website that is live and collecting enquiries this month is worth more than a perfect one that launches in four months.

## Red flags when comparing website quotes

- **No written scope.** If the quote does not list pages, features and revisions, you cannot hold anyone to it.
- **"Lifetime free hosting".** Someone is paying for that server. Ask who owns it and what happens if you leave.
- **No mobile preview.** Most of your visitors in Delhi NCR will come from a phone on 4G. Ask to see the mobile version of past work.
- **No SEO basics.** If page titles, descriptions and Google Search Console are not mentioned, assume they are not included.
- **Prices without GST clarity.** Website design by a GST-registered agency is normally billed at 18% GST. A ₹30,000 quote "plus GST" is ₹35,400. This is general information, not tax advice.

## Freelancer, agency or DIY builder?

| Option | Upfront cost | Best when | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| DIY website builder | Low | You have time and only need a simple page | Your own time, monthly plan fees, limited SEO control |
| Freelancer | Low to medium | Small, clear scope and a trusted reference | Availability after launch, domain ownership |
| Agency | Medium to high | You need design, content, SEO and ads tracking together | Paying for features you don't need |

There is no single right answer. What matters is that someone is accountable for the site after launch, when you need a price changed or a new offer page for Diwali.

## A simple checklist before you pay

- [ ] Number of pages and their names written in the quote
- [ ] Who writes the content, and in which languages
- [ ] Mobile-first design, with a mobile preview before launch
- [ ] WhatsApp button and enquiry form that reach your phone
- [ ] Basic on-page SEO, sitemap and Google Search Console setup
- [ ] Google Business Profile linked to the website
- [ ] Domain and hosting in your name, with logins shared
- [ ] Yearly renewal costs listed
- [ ] GST shown clearly
- [ ] Timeline, and what happens if it slips
