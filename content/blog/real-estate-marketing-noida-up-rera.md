Noida is one of the most competitive property markets in India. Dozens of projects across Greater Noida West, the Noida Expressway, Sector 150 and the Yamuna Expressway target the same buyers on the same apps. Buyers have seen every "limited inventory" ad, and UP RERA watches advertising closely. **Real estate marketing in Noida** now comes down to two things: staying compliant, and being more specific than everyone else.

This playbook is for developers, channel partners and brokers. It covers UP RERA ad rules, how to split campaigns by micro-market, and how to turn leads into site visits. Rules are as of November 2026; this is general information, not legal advice.

## UP RERA rules every Noida ad must follow

Noida, Greater Noida and the Yamuna Expressway area fall under **UP RERA**. Under the RERA Act and UP RERA's directions:

- **Register first.** A project that needs registration cannot be advertised, marketed, booked or sold before it is registered. That includes "pre-launch", "EOI" and "soft launch" campaigns.
- **Show the registration number** and the UP RERA website ([up-rera.in](https://www.up-rera.in)) on every ad, landing page, brochure and hoarding.
- **Show the project QR code.** UP RERA has required QR codes on project advertisements since 2024, and in May 2025 it issued further guidelines on prominently displaying registration details and the QR code in promotional material.
- **Agents need their own registration.** Channel partners and brokers should be registered with UP RERA and show their agent registration number.
- **Keep claims accurate.** Prices, carpet area, amenities, approvals and possession dates must match what is registered. "Assured returns" and vague "near metro" or "near airport" claims invite complaints.

### A compliance checklist for each creative

| Item | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| UP RERA project registration number | Ad image or video end card, landing page header or footer, brochure |
| Project QR code | Static ads, brochure, hoardings, landing page |
| up-rera.in | Next to the registration number |
| Agent registration number (if a channel partner) | Ad copy or creative, landing page |
| Price "starting from" with what it includes | Ad copy and landing page, matching each other |
| Possession date as registered | Landing page and brochure |

Get the developer to approve every channel-partner creative before it goes live. Both the promoter and the agent can face action for a wrong ad.

## Real estate marketing in Noida by micro-market

Noida is not one market. A buyer comparing 2 BHKs in Greater Noida West thinks differently from someone looking at luxury flats on the Expressway or plots near Jewar. Run separate campaigns, landing pages and creatives for each.

| Micro-market | Typical buyer | What to lead with |
|---|---|---|
| Greater Noida West (Noida Extension) | First-time buyers, young families, IT professionals | Ready or near-ready inventory, EMI, schools, possession record |
| Noida–Greater Noida Expressway sectors | Upgraders, senior professionals | Location, specifications, green areas, builder track record |
| Sector 150 and nearby | Premium end-users and investors | Open space, sports facilities, low density |
| Central Noida sectors (resale and new) | Families wanting established areas | Metro access, markets, hospitals |
| Yamuna Expressway (YEIDA area) | Investors and plot buyers | Approvals, allotment status, long-term infrastructure, with careful wording |

Around the Yamuna Expressway, buyers ask about the Noida International Airport at Jewar and the film city plans. Quote only official, current status and avoid "guaranteed appreciation" lines.

## Channels and campaign set-up

### Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram)

Meta usually brings the most volume in Noida. What works:

- **Walkthrough reels** of the actual sample flat or construction site, with the RERA number on the end card
- **Instant Forms with qualifying questions**: budget range, BHK, timeline to buy, and whether they want a site visit this week
- **Separate ad sets by micro-market** and by buyer type (end-user vs investor)
- **Retargeting** of video viewers and site visitors with floor plans and payment plans

As of November 2026, Meta's Housing Special Ad Category applies to ads aimed at the US, Canada and parts of Europe, not India, but Meta's general ad standards still apply.

### Google Ads

Google Search catches people already deep in research:

- Project and builder name searches (bid on your own brand so brokers don't take it)
- Sector-based searches such as "2 BHK in Greater Noida West" or "3 BHK Sector 150"
- Call ads during evenings and weekends, when buyers have time

Add negatives for rent, PG, jobs and "resale" if you only sell new inventory. See our [Google Ads service](/services/google-ads/) for how we structure these.

### Video and AI explainers

A two-minute site walkthrough, a drone shot of the location (with permissions) and a short payment-plan explainer answer most questions before the first call. [AI avatar videos](/blog/ai-avatar-videos-for-real-estate-and-solar/) can help with multilingual explainers for NRI buyers.

## From lead to site visit

Most Noida leads are lost in the first hour because the buyer has enquired on three projects at once. A simple process:

1. **WhatsApp within five minutes** with the brochure, RERA number, location pin and two site-visit slots.
2. **Call within 15 minutes** during working hours and confirm budget and timeline.
3. **Book the visit on that call**, with a weekend option and pick-up if you offer it.
4. **Remind the day before and two hours before.**
5. **Follow up after the visit** with the specific unit discussed and a cost sheet.
6. **Re-engage cold leads** at 7, 21 and 45 days with new construction updates.

Score leads by budget, timeline and visit status so sales teams call the best ones first. Track cost per site visit and per booking, not just cost per lead. Our [WhatsApp AI automation guide](/blog/whatsapp-ai-automation-lead-follow-up/) shows how to automate the first steps.
