Madhya Pradesh is one of the most interesting growth markets for solar and real estate businesses. Bhopal is a fast-growing capital city, Rewa is known across India for the Rewa Ultra Mega Solar park, and Satna is an industrial and cement hub. Advertising costs are generally lower than in Delhi NCR, but the way buyers decide is different.

Here is what works in these three cities.

## What's different about MP buyers

- **Language:** most buyers prefer Hindi or Hinglish. A polished English ad often feels distant.
- **Trust:** people want to know who you are. Local reputation, office address and real photos matter more than slick design.
- **WhatsApp first:** many buyers would rather chat than fill a long form or visit a website.
- **Family decisions:** property and solar are family purchases. Your follow-up should be easy to forward on WhatsApp.

## Solar: education beats hard selling

Many homeowners in Bhopal, Rewa and Satna have heard of PM Surya Ghar but don't know the steps. Ads that explain simply in Hindi perform well:

- "₹2,000 का बिजली बिल? सरकारी सब्सिडी से 3 kW सोलर लगवाएं" (check the latest subsidy before using numbers)
- A 30-second reel showing the real process: survey, installation, net meter, subsidy credit
- Customer stories from the same city, with the customer's permission and their real before-and-after bills

DISCOM matters for follow-up: Bhopal is served by MP Madhya Kshetra Vidyut Vitaran Company, while Rewa and Satna are under MP Poorv Kshetra Vidyut Vitaran Company. Mention the right one when explaining net metering.

**Commercial and farm solar** are big in this region too. Satna's industrial belt suits commercial rooftop campaigns, and rural areas around Rewa and Satna have demand for solar pumps under PM-KUSUM, which needs a different message and audience.

## Real estate: plots, houses and proof

In Bhopal, demand is spread across corridors like Kolar Road, Narmadapuram (Hoshangabad) Road, Ayodhya Bypass and Misrod. In Rewa and Satna, much of the demand is for residential plots, independent houses and small commercial spaces.

What converts:

- **Drone or walk-through videos** of the actual site, roads and nearby landmarks
- **Approvals front and centre:** MP RERA number, TNCP and other approvals where applicable, clear title
- **Price clarity:** "Plots from ₹X per sq ft" works better than "Call for price" in smaller markets
- **Site-visit offers:** free pickup and drop for site visits on weekends

## Targeting tips for smaller cities

- Use a **radius** around the city and include nearby towns (for example Mauganj, Sirmour and Teonthar around Rewa; Maihar, Amarpatan and Nagod around Satna).
- Keep audiences broad; small cities don't have enough people for narrow interest targeting.
- Run **one campaign per city**, not one for "MP".
- Retarget video viewers with proof and offers.

## Google: small but valuable

Search volume in Rewa and Satna is lower than in Bhopal, but searches like "solar company in Rewa" or "plots in Satna" are high intent. Keep a small Search budget running and make sure your **Google Business Profile** is complete, with photos and reviews, because many searches end on Maps.

## WhatsApp is your storefront

Set up WhatsApp Business with a catalogue, quick replies in Hindi, and an automatic greeting. Every ad should offer WhatsApp as the first option. Read [WhatsApp and AI automation for lead follow-up](/blog/whatsapp-ai-automation-lead-follow-up/) for the full setup.

## A starter plan for MP businesses

1. Shoot 5 to 6 real videos in Hindi: founder intro, site or installation, customer story, process, FAQ.
2. Launch Meta lead and WhatsApp campaigns per city with a modest daily budget.
3. Run a small Google Search campaign for local intent.
4. Complete your Google Business Profile and ask happy customers for reviews.
5. Reply to every lead within 10 minutes.
