Video is the format that sells on Instagram, Facebook and YouTube, but shooting a new video every time a price, offer or subsidy changes is slow and expensive. AI avatar videos solve that problem for many solar and real estate businesses.

## What is an AI avatar video?

An AI avatar video shows a digital presenter speaking your script. The presenter can be:

- **A stock avatar** from the tool's library, or
- **A custom avatar of a real person** (for example, the founder or sales head), created from a short recording **with their written consent**.

You type or upload the script, choose the language and voice, and the tool generates a lip-synced video. Editors then add captions, brand colours, B-roll and music.

## Where AI avatars work well

### For solar companies

- **Subsidy explainers:** "How PM Surya Ghar subsidy works in 45 seconds", updated whenever rules change
- **Process videos:** survey to installation to net metering to subsidy credit
- **FAQ series:** warranty, maintenance, monsoon performance, loan options
- **WhatsApp follow-up:** a short personal-style video sent after a lead comes in

### For builders and brokers

- **Project introductions** in Hindi and English
- **Corridor explainers** (for example, why Dwarka Expressway or Kolar Road)
- **Price, offer and possession updates**
- **NRI updates** that can be sent across timezones
- **Channel partner training** on project details

## Where real footage still wins

Use real video for anything that proves you are real: site walkthroughs, sample flats, installation teams on a roof, customer testimonials and founder stories. The strongest ads usually combine both: a real opening shot to build trust, then an avatar explaining the details.

## A simple production workflow

1. **Brief:** goal, audience, platform and call to action
2. **Script:** a hook in the first three seconds, one main idea, and a clear next step (WhatsApp or call). Keep it 30 to 60 seconds.
3. **Fact check:** subsidy amounts, prices, RERA numbers, possession dates
4. **Generate:** choose avatar, language and voice; generate 2 or 3 hook variations
5. **Edit:** captions (most people watch without sound), brand frame, real B-roll, music
6. **Approve:** the business owner signs off on every claim
7. **Publish and test:** run variations as separate ads and keep the winners

## Mistakes to avoid

- **No consent.** Never create an avatar of a real person without written permission.
- **Fake testimonials.** Don't use avatars to pretend to be customers. It's misleading and can break platform rules and consumer protection law.
- **Robotic scripts.** Write the way people speak. Hinglish often sounds more natural than formal Hindi.
- **Mispronounced names.** Check place names, project names and numbers before publishing.
- **Skipping disclosure where required.** Platforms increasingly ask advertisers to label realistic AI-generated or altered content; follow their current rules.

## How to measure results

Judge avatar videos the same way as any ad: hook rate (3-second views), hold rate, cost per lead and cost per qualified lead. Avatar explainers often suit retargeting and follow-up, while real-footage hooks often do better with cold audiences, but results vary by business, so test both in your own account.
