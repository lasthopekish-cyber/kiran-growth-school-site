*This article is general information, not legal advice. Always check your state RERA's current rules or ask a lawyer.*

Under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, registered projects must be advertised honestly and with their registration details. State authorities have become stricter about digital ads, and some have published specific advertising directions. If you run Meta or Google ads for property in Delhi NCR or Madhya Pradesh, here's what to get right.

## Which RERA applies where

| Market | Authority |
|---|---|
| Delhi | Delhi RERA |
| Gurugram, Faridabad | Haryana RERA (HRERA Gurugram / HRERA Panchkula) |
| Ghaziabad, Noida, Greater Noida | UP RERA |
| Bhopal, Rewa, Satna | MP RERA |

## The core checklist

### 1. Registration number and website, clearly visible

Show the project's RERA registration number and the RERA website address on:

- Every image and video ad (readable on a phone screen, not hidden in tiny text)
- The ad's primary text where possible
- Landing pages and project microsites (near the top, not only in the footer)
- Brochures, price sheets and WhatsApp creatives

UP RERA, for example, has issued standard directions on how registration details should appear in project advertisements, and MahaRERA requires a QR code alongside the registration number. Expect other states to move in the same direction.

### 2. No ads before registration

Projects that need registration should not be advertised for bookings before they are registered. "Coming soon" teasers that collect bookings or token amounts are risky.

### 3. Accurate, verifiable claims

- **Prices:** if you show a price, make clear what it includes (basic price, charges, taxes).
- **Area:** use carpet area as defined under RERA, not only super area.
- **Possession date:** match the date in the RERA registration.
- **Amenities and approvals:** only show what is approved and planned.
- **Distances:** use realistic drive times, not "5 minutes from the airport" if it's 25.

### 4. Agent registration

Real estate agents and channel partners generally need their own RERA agent registration in the state where they sell. Partners should show their agent registration where required.

## Platform rules to know

**Meta (Facebook and Instagram):** In some regions such as the US, Canada and parts of Europe, housing ads must use a Special Ad Category with restricted targeting. India is not on that list today, but Meta's general advertising standards still apply: no misleading claims, no discrimination, and accurate landing pages.

**Google Ads:** ads must not be misleading, landing pages must match the ad, and trademark use in ad text is restricted. Some financial claims (loans, guaranteed returns) attract extra scrutiny.

## Channel partner ads: a simple process

Most compliance problems come from partner ads with changed prices or missing RERA numbers. A clean process:

1. The developer shares an **approved creative kit** with RERA details already on every asset.
2. Partners can change only their own contact details and agent registration.
3. Partners use their own landing pages, which also show RERA details.
4. The developer keeps a list of approved partners and checks ads in the Meta Ad Library from time to time.

## Avoid "guaranteed returns" language

Phrases like "assured 12% returns" or "double your money" can create legal and platform problems. If an investment scheme is genuinely offered, its terms must be documented and accurate. Usually it's safer to talk about rental demand, connectivity and quality.

## Compliance is also good marketing

Buyers in Gurugram, Ghaziabad and Bhopal increasingly check RERA before they visit. A clear RERA number, honest price and real possession date build trust and improve lead quality.
