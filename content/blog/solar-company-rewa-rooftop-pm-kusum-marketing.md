## Solar company in Rewa: the rooftop (PM Surya Ghar) pitch

As of November 2026, the central subsidy under PM Surya Ghar is ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third kW: ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and a maximum of ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above. Union Minister Pralhad Joshi said in November 2026 that around 66 lakh households had been solarised under the scheme. Point buyers to [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in) so they can check figures themselves.

Rewa is served by **MPPoKVVCL**, the East Zone DISCOM. Explain the process in five plain steps on your landing page and in WhatsApp replies:

1. **Apply** on the PM Surya Ghar portal, choose Madhya Pradesh and the East Zone DISCOM, and enter the consumer number from the bill.
2. **Feasibility** check by the DISCOM on sanctioned load and transformer capacity.
3. **Installation** by a vendor registered on the portal, using ALMM-listed panels.
4. **Inspection and net meter** by the DISCOM after the installer uploads documents.
5. **Subsidy** credited to the homeowner's bank account after commissioning.

Timelines depend on DISCOM workload and documents, so give a range rather than a promise. Tell buyers to keep their latest bill, Aadhaar, a cancelled cheque and property papers ready. Net metering and export rules are set by MPERC and can change; check the current position before putting numbers in ads. This is general information, not legal or financial advice.

Never say "free solar panels" or "zero bill guaranteed". Say "subsidy up to ₹78,000" and "savings depend on your usage".

## Marketing solar pumps to farmers honestly

This is where Rewa installers can stand out, because the scheme picture is confusing right now:

- **PM-KUSUM phase 1** closed to new applications on 31 March 2026. Sanctioned standalone pump projects had a commissioning deadline of 30 September 2026.
- **PM-KUSUM 2.0** had been proposed but, as of early November 2026, had not been approved by the Union Cabinet, so its subsidy rates and application windows are not final.
- **Madhya Pradesh** runs its own **PM Krishak Mitra Surya Yojana** through MPUVNL. In November 2025 the state Cabinet approved support of up to 90% of the cost for eligible farmers, with priority for temporary pump connection holders.

There is also a trust problem. A 101Reporters investigation published in August 2026 found that some farmers in other MP districts did not realise part of their pump cost was a bank loan in their name. If you sell pumps, explain the full cost structure in writing: what the government pays, what the farmer pays upfront, whether any part is a loan, and who repays it.

Marketing that works with farmers in Rewa:

- **Hindi or Bagheli video** of a real pump you installed, with the farmer explaining in his own words (with consent)
- **"Scheme status" updates** on WhatsApp: what is open, what is not, and how to check on the official portal
- **Document readiness drives**: Khasra and land records, Aadhaar seeding, bank details. These help farmers whatever scheme opens next
- **Village meetings** with the sarpanch or a farmer producer organisation, not just ads
- **Clear disclaimers**: "Subsidy subject to scheme approval and eligibility. Check MPUVNL for current status."

Installers who stay honest during a gap in the scheme are the ones farmers call when the next phase opens.
