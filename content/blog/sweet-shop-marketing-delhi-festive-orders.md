Delhi eats, gifts and celebrates with mithai, but the order calendar is lumpy. A shop that is quiet in a normal week can be overwhelmed in the ten days before Diwali or Raksha Bandhan. Good **sweet shop marketing in Delhi** is about two things: being easy to find and order from every day, and planning each festival early enough to win bulk and corporate orders before your competitors do.

This guide is for sweet shops, halwais and mithai brands in Delhi, from family shops in Chandni Chowk and Karol Bagh to newer outlets in Dwarka, Rohini and South Delhi. With Diwali 2026 just behind us, this is the right moment to plan for the wedding season and next year's festive calendar.

## The Delhi sweet shop festival calendar

Plan campaigns around the moments when people buy most. Exact festival dates change every year, so check the calendar and work backwards.

| Occasion | Who buys | Start promoting | What to push |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wedding season (roughly Nov to Feb) | Families, caterers, event planners | Ongoing, 4 to 6 weeks before peak muhurat dates | Bulk orders, shagun boxes, tasting appointments |
| Lohri and Makar Sankranti (January) | Families, Punjabi households | 2 to 3 weeks before | Gajak, rewari, til laddoo, gift packs |
| Holi (March) | Families, offices | 3 weeks before | Gujiya, thandai, combo packs |
| Raksha Bandhan (August) | Sisters, outstation senders | 3 to 4 weeks before | Rakhi + sweet combos, courier delivery |
| Diwali and Bhai Dooj (October/November) | Families, corporates, RWAs | 8 to 10 weeks before (corporate), 4 to 6 weeks (retail) | Dry fruit boxes, sugar-free range, branded corporate boxes |
| Everyday | Walk-ins, birthdays, puja | Always on | Fresh daily items, small boxes, namkeen |

## Sweet shop marketing in Delhi: the five channels that matter

### 1. Google Business Profile and Maps

People search "sweet shop near me", "best mithai in Lajpat Nagar" or "kaju katli Rohini" and decide in seconds. Your Google Business Profile should have:

- Correct name, address, phone and festive hours (update them before every festival)
- Real photos of the counter, gift boxes and fresh batches, refreshed often
- Products with prices, including gift boxes
- Replies to every review, good and bad
- A link to your WhatsApp or ordering page

If you have more than one outlet, each needs its own verified listing.

### 2. Instagram and Facebook reels

Mithai is visual. Short reels of ghee being poured, jalebi being fried or a gift box being packed are naturally engaging. A simple weekly rhythm works: one making-of reel, one gift box reel, and one customer or festival reel. Captions in Hinglish usually feel most natural for Delhi audiences.

### 3. Local Meta Ads

Organic reach is limited, so use small, local [Meta Ads](/services/meta-ads/) campaigns around each festival. Target a 3 to 8 km radius around each outlet, use your best-performing reels as ads, and send people to WhatsApp rather than a long form. Budgets depend on the number of outlets and the season; start small, see which reels bring chats, then increase.

### 4. WhatsApp ordering and repeat customers

WhatsApp Business is the cheapest ordering system a sweet shop can have:

- Catalogue with box sizes, weights and prices
- Quick replies for delivery areas, cut-off times and payment
- A broadcast list (opted-in customers only) for festival menus
- A label for corporate and bulk buyers so you can contact them early next year

### 5. Corporate and bulk gifting outreach

Corporate orders are large but decided early. Build a one-page gifting menu with price tiers, branding options (sleeves, cards, logo stickers), minimum quantities and delivery coverage across Delhi NCR. Share it with HR and admin teams, RWAs and event planners well before Diwali, and follow up by phone.

## Trust and compliance in your marketing

Delhi buyers have become careful about quality, especially at festival time. Make trust visible:

- **FSSAI licence number** on your website, boxes and Google profile
- **Best before dates** clearly shown. FSSAI has required "best before" dates to be displayed for loose sweets since October 2020, so show this on your counter trays and in reel captions too
- **Ingredient claims you can back up**: say "made in desi ghee" only if it is true for that product
- **Sugar-free and allergen labels** where relevant

This is general information, not legal advice. Check current FSSAI rules for your products.

## A simple 6-week festive plan

| Week | Action |
|---|---|
| 6 weeks before | Finalise gift box range and prices; photograph every box; update Google profile and WhatsApp catalogue |
| 5 weeks before | Send corporate gifting menu to past buyers and new leads |
| 4 weeks before | Start making-of and gift box reels; launch small local Meta Ads |
| 3 weeks before | Pre-order offer for early birds; WhatsApp broadcast to opted-in customers |
| 2 weeks before | Increase ad budget on reels that bring chats; post delivery cut-off dates |
| Final week | Daily stories on fresh batches; festive hours on Google; thank-you message after the festival |

After the festival, note which boxes sold out, which reels brought orders and which corporate clients ordered. That list is your head start for next year.

## Do you need a website?

A WhatsApp catalogue and Google profile can carry a small shop. A website helps once you sell gift boxes across Delhi NCR, take corporate orders, or want to rank for searches like "Diwali sweets box Delhi". Keep it simple: menu, gift boxes, corporate gifting page, outlet locations and a WhatsApp order button.
