## What a website designer in Satna should build for you

A good local business website is not a digital brochure. It does four jobs:

1. **Answers the searcher's question fast**: what you do, where you are, how to contact you.
2. **Builds trust**: real photos, owner name, years in business, genuine Google reviews.
3. **Ranks on Google** for your products and services plus "Satna" and nearby areas.
4. **Captures the enquiry** through click-to-call, WhatsApp and a short form.

Everything else is decoration. Before you pay any advance, ask how the site will do each of these four jobs.

### For traders: a catalogue beats a brochure

Many Satna traders only need a clean product catalogue, not an online store. Show categories, brands and pack sizes, and put a "Get rate on WhatsApp" button next to each one. That keeps prices flexible while still capturing the enquiry. Add your GST number and full address on the contact page; B2B buyers look for both.

### For schools: plan around admission season

Admission searches peak between roughly December and April. Get the site live well before that, with an admissions page that answers the questions parents actually ask: classes offered, board, transport, timings, documents needed and how to visit. ## Local SEO checklist for Satna businesses

A website alone does not rank. Work through this list after launch:

- **Google Business Profile** claimed, verified and linked to the website, with the right category
- **Same name, address and phone** on the website, Google, Justdial, IndiaMART and Facebook
- **One page per main service or product line**, each mentioning Satna and the areas you serve
- **Landmarks** customers use, such as Rewa Road, Maihar Road, Sindhi Camp or near the railway station
- **Local business schema** added by your designer
- **Google Search Console** set up so you can see which searches show your site
- **Fast mobile loading**: compressed images, no autoplay video, ideally under about 3 seconds on 4G
- **Hindi headings and descriptions** on key pages
- **A steady flow of genuine Google reviews**; ask every happy customer

If you serve customers outside the city, add short sections for Maihar, Nagod, Amarpatan or Unchehara, but only where you actually deliver or have customers. Thin pages that just swap the town name do more harm than good.

## Mistakes we see on Satna business websites

- **Domain registered in the designer's name.** If you fall out, you can lose your website. Insist on your own email as the account owner.
- **No phone number above the fold.** Most visitors are on mobile and want to call.
- **Stock photos only.** Real photos of your shop, godown, school campus or team build far more trust.
- **Auto-translated Hindi.** It reads badly and puts people off. Get it written properly.
- **Site never updated.** Old offers and wrong timings tell Google and customers the business is inactive.

## How to choose a website designer in Satna

Ask every designer these questions before paying:

1. Can I see three live websites you have built, on my phone?
2. Will the domain and hosting be in my name?
3. How many pages, revisions and who writes the content?
4. Do you set up Google Business Profile linking, Search Console and schema?
5. Can you write good Hindi content?
6. What will I pay each year after the first year?
7. How fast can you update prices, photos or notices later?
