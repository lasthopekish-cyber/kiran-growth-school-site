A year ago, most small businesses thought of AI video as a gimmick. Now it is a practical tool. **AI video making services** can turn a script into a finished explainer, an offer reel or ten ad variations in a fraction of the time a shoot takes. But AI video is not always the right choice, and a poor one can make your brand look cheap.

This guide explains what AI video making involves, what it costs in India as of November 2026, which tools are used, the labelling rules to follow, and when it beats a camera shoot.

## What AI video making services actually do

"AI video" covers several different things. A good service mixes them depending on the job:

| Type | What it is | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| AI-generated visuals | Scenes or clips created from text or image prompts | Concept shots, backgrounds, product-in-use scenes |
| AI avatar or presenter | A digital presenter speaking your script | Explainers, FAQs, multilingual updates |
| AI voiceover | Natural voice in Hindi, English or Hinglish | Narration over product or site footage |
| AI-assisted editing | Auto-captions, cut detection, resizing, B-roll suggestions | Faster edits of your own footage |
| Motion graphics from templates | Animated text, icons and data | Offers, steps, price comparisons |

Most strong results come from **combining** these with some real footage, for example your own shop photos animated with AI motion, a human-written script and an AI voiceover in Hindi.

## When AI video beats a shoot

- **You need volume.** Ads work best with many creative variations. AI makes it practical to test five hooks instead of one.
- **You need several languages.** One script can become Hindi, English and Hinglish versions quickly.
- **The message changes often.** Offers, prices, admission dates and festive deals can be updated without reshooting.
- **The subject is a process or idea.** "How the PM Surya Ghar subsidy works" or "Steps to book a site visit" are easier to explain with graphics than with a camera.
- **You have no time or space for a shoot.** Small shops and solo professionals often cannot stop work for a day.

## When a real shoot is better

- **Testimonials.** A real customer speaking builds trust that AI cannot fake, and should not try to.
- **Your real premises or work.** Buyers want to see the actual school, shop, project site or installation.
- **Food and products.** Sweets, cakes and products sell on texture and close-ups; real footage wins.
- **Your face and team.** For local businesses, a short clip of the owner speaking is often the best-performing video.

## Rules to follow: labels, consent and honesty

AI video is powerful, so use it carefully:

- **Labelling.** India amended its IT Rules in February 2026 to require labels on synthetically generated content, and Meta and YouTube apply their own AI disclosure labels. Disclose when realistic people, voices or scenes are AI-generated.
- **Consent.** Never clone a real person's face or voice without their written consent. Never imitate a celebrity or public figure.
- **No fake proof.** Do not create AI "customers", fake testimonials or fake site footage. It can break platform policies and consumer protection rules, and it destroys trust when found out.
- **Real estate and solar.** Any claims, prices, RERA numbers or subsidy figures in the video must be accurate and current.

This is general information, not legal advice.

## How to brief an AI video so it doesn't look generic

1. **One message per video.** One offer, one question, one step.
2. **Hook in the first two seconds.** A question or number your customer cares about.
3. **Use your real brand assets.** Logo, colours, real photos of your shop or work.
4. **Write the script in your customer's language.** Simple Hindi or Hinglish often beats polished English.
5. **End with one clear action.** "WhatsApp us for a quote" with your number on screen.
6. **Make 3–5 variations** of the hook for ads and keep the winners.
