Search "website designer Noida" and you will see directories, freelancer platforms and dozens of agencies all claiming to be the best. Ratings help, but they do not tell you whether a designer will hand over your domain, set up SEO properly or build a site that brings enquiries. If you want the **best website designer in Noida** for your business, the quickest filter is a set of plain questions asked before you pay an advance.

Below are 12 questions we would ask if we were hiring a designer ourselves, why each one matters, and what a good answer sounds like. They work whether you run a clinic in Sector 50, a coaching centre in Sector 62, a showroom in Noida Extension or a factory in Phase 2.

## The 12 questions to ask the best website designer in Noida

### Ownership and money

**1. Will the domain and hosting be in my name?**
Good answer: "Yes, registered with your email. We can manage it, but you own the logins." If the designer insists on keeping the domain, walk away. Recovering a domain later is slow and stressful.

**2. What exactly is in the quote, and what costs extra?**
Ask for a written list: number of pages, who writes the content, revisions, contact forms, WhatsApp button, SEO setup and training. Our guide to [website design cost in Delhi NCR](/blog/website-design-cost-delhi-ncr/) lists typical 2026 price ranges if you want a benchmark.

**3. What will I pay every year after launch?**
Domain renewal, hosting, SSL, business email and small updates all recur. A designer who mentions these upfront is being honest with you.

**4. Is GST included?**
Agency invoices usually carry 18% GST. Compare quotes on the same basis. This is general information, not tax advice.

### Proof and process

**5. Can I see three live websites you built, on my phone?**
Screenshots and Behance mock-ups are not enough. Open the live sites on mobile data, not office Wi-Fi. Check that they load quickly, the text is readable and the call button works.

**6. Who will actually build my site?**
Some agencies sell and then pass the work to someone else. That is not always bad, but you should know who to call when something breaks.

**7. What do you need from me, and by when?**
Most delays come from content: text, photos, logo, service list, prices. A good designer gives you a simple checklist on day one. With content ready, a standard business site can go live very quickly; without it, even simple sites drag for weeks.

### Getting found on Google

**8. What SEO setup is included?**
At minimum: a unique title and meta description for every page, one clear H1, image alt text, an XML sitemap, Google Search Console, and LocalBusiness schema with your name, address and phone. Many cheap quotes skip all of this.

**9. Will you create pages for the areas I serve?**
Noida buyers search by area: "Sector 18", "Greater Noida West", "Noida Extension", "Sector 137". Useful area or service pages, written properly rather than copied with only the place name changed, help you show up for those searches.

**10. Will the site connect with my Google Business Profile?**
For local businesses, the Maps listing often brings more calls than the website itself. The website and Google Business Profile should show the same name, address, phone and hours, and link to each other.

### Leads and speed

**11. How will I know if the website brings enquiries?**
Ask for WhatsApp click tracking, call tracking, form notifications to your phone and Google Analytics. If you plan to run ads, the Meta Pixel and Google tag should be installed and tested.

**12. How fast will it load on a normal phone?**
Ask them to run Google PageSpeed Insights on one of their live sites in front of you. Heavy sliders, autoplay videos and uncompressed photos are common reasons sites feel slow on mobile.

## A quick scoring table

Use this to compare two or three designers side by side.

| Question area | Red flag | Good sign |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership | Domain in designer's name | Domain and hosting in your name |
| Scope | "Everything included" with no list | Written list of pages and features |
| Yearly costs | Not mentioned | Itemised renewal and upkeep |
| Proof | Only screenshots | Live sites that load fast on mobile |
| SEO | "We do SEO later" | Titles, schema, sitemap, Search Console at launch |
| Local search | No area pages, no Maps link | Area pages and Google Business Profile linked |
| Leads | No tracking | WhatsApp, call and form tracking set up |
| Support | No contact after launch | A named person and response time |

If a designer scores well on six or more rows, you are probably in good hands. If they dodge ownership or tracking, keep looking, however good the design looks.

## What Noida businesses usually need on their website

Every business is different, but most Noida websites we plan include:

- **Home page** that says what you do, where, and for whom, in the first screen
- **Service pages**, one per main service, so each can rank on its own
- **Area or location section** for the sectors and societies you serve
- **About page** with real photos of your team and premises
- **Contact page** with map, hours, click-to-call and WhatsApp
- **Trust signals**: registrations, certifications, real reviews (never invented ones)
- **Hindi or Hinglish** lines where your buyers prefer them

Industry sites need more. A solar installer needs a savings calculator and subsidy explainer; a real estate project needs RERA details on every page; a coaching centre needs batch timings and a demo-class form. ## Mistakes to avoid when hiring

- **Choosing on price alone.** A ₹5,000 site that nobody finds costs more in lost enquiries than a well-built one.
- **Paying 100% advance.** A fair split is part advance, part on design approval, balance on launch.
- **Skipping the content plan.** Decide your services, prices and photos before the designer starts.
- **Ignoring mobile.** Most local searches in Noida happen on phones. Approve the design on a phone, not a laptop.
- **No handover.** Ask for all logins and a short training session on how to update text and photos.
