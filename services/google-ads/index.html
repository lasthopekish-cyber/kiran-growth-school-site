Is Google Ads better than Meta Ads for solar?+

They do different jobs. Google catches people already searching, so leads are high intent but limited in volume. Meta creates demand. Most installers do best with both.

Can you track phone calls from ads?+

Yes. We set up call extensions, call conversion tracking and WhatsApp click events so you see exactly which keywords bring calls.

Do you need access to my Google Ads account?+

We work inside your own account so the data and history always stay yours.
