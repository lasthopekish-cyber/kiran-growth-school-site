What is the best way to get solar leads in India?+

Combine Google Search ads for people already searching for rooftop solar, Meta lead and WhatsApp ads to create demand around PM Surya Ghar savings, a fast website with a savings calculator, and instant WhatsApp follow-up. Qualify every lead by monthly bill, roof ownership and DISCOM.

How much subsidy does PM Surya Ghar give?+

Central financial assistance is 60% of system cost up to 2 kW and 40% of the additional cost from 2 to 3 kW, capped at ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above (about ₹30,000 for 1 kW and ₹60,000 for 2 kW). Some states add extra support. Always confirm current figures on the official PM Surya Ghar portal.

Can you run ads for both residential and commercial solar?+

Yes. Residential PM Surya Ghar campaigns, housing society (RWA) campaigns and commercial and industrial rooftop campaigns are run separately because buyers, budgets and messages are different.

Do you work with solar EPCs in Madhya Pradesh?+

Yes, including Bhopal, Rewa and Satna, with Hindi-first creatives and campaigns that cover nearby towns.
