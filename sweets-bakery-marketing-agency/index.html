Why sweets & bakery marketing often underperforms

Great products, weak reels

Most bakery and mithai shop content is a static photo. We create mouth-watering reels from your real kitchen and counter that people save and share.

Festival rush not planned

Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, Christmas and wedding season decide your year. We plan gift-box and pre-order campaigns weeks in advance.

Invisible on Google Maps

When someone searches 'cake shop near me' or 'best sweets near me', a weak Google Business Profile loses the sale. We fix photos, categories, menus and reviews.

Orders lost in DMs

Cake and box orders arrive in Instagram DMs, calls and WhatsApp with no system. We set up a WhatsApp ordering flow and catalogue so no order is missed.

Ask on WhatsApp
How can a bakery get more orders online?+

Post short reels of products being made and served, run local Meta Ads within a few kilometres of the shop, keep Google Business Profile photos and menu updated, collect reviews, and make ordering easy with a WhatsApp catalogue.

When should a sweet shop start Diwali marketing?+

Start teasing gift boxes and corporate orders 4 to 6 weeks before Diwali, open pre-orders 2 to 3 weeks before, and push same-day and pickup messaging in the final week.

Do you shoot the product videos?+

We edit footage you shoot on your phone using our simple shooting guide, keep the real look of your products without heavy filters, and add captions and your logo. On-site shoots can be arranged in some cities.

Can you help us rank on Google Maps?+

Yes. We optimise your Google Business Profile (categories, photos, products, posts and review requests) and build a fast website and local pages that support your Maps ranking.
