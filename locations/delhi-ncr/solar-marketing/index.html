Which DISCOM handles net metering in Delhi NCR?+

In Delhi NCR the DISCOM(s) are BSES, Tata Power-DDL, DHBVN (Gurugram, Faridabad), PVVNL (Ghaziabad, Noida). Net-metering steps and timelines differ by DISCOM, so we explain the exact process in your ads and follow-up messages. Always confirm current steps with the DISCOM and the PM Surya Ghar portal.

How much subsidy can my customers get under PM Surya Ghar?+

Central financial assistance is capped at ₹78,000 per household for systems of 3 kW and above (about ₹30,000 for 1 kW and ₹60,000 for 2 kW). Some states add extra support. Confirm current figures on pmsuryaghar.gov.in before advertising them.

Do you run commercial and RWA solar campaigns in Delhi NCR?+

Yes. Housing society (RWA), commercial and industrial rooftop campaigns run separately from residential PM Surya Ghar campaigns because the buyers, budgets and decision process are different.
