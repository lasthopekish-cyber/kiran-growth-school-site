Quick answer

A solar company in Delhi gets better leads by splitting campaigns by DISCOM area (BSES Rajdhani, BSES Yamuna, Tata Power-DDL and NDMC), speaking to small and shared rooftops, and explaining the PM Surya Ghar subsidy (up to ₹78,000 for 3 kW) plus Delhi's own state support clearly and accurately. Delhi is one of India's toughest markets for a solar company in Delhi to advertise in. Ad costs are high, rooftops are small and often shared, and buyers hear five different subsidy numbers from five different installers. The installers who win here are the ones who sound most local and most accurate: they know which DISCOM the buyer is with, what the roof can actually take, and what the subsidy really is today.

This guide explains how to structure rooftop solar marketing across Delhi's DISCOM areas. Subsidy and policy points are as of November 2026; confirm on official portals before you quote.

Delhi's DISCOM map and why it matters for marketing

Delhi homes are supplied by different distribution companies depending on location. Each has its own solar and net metering application process, so the buyer's first practical question is often "who handles my meter?"

DISCOM Broad areas served What to put in your ads and landing page
BSES Rajdhani (BRPL) South and West Delhi Steps and documents for BRPL net metering, local project photos
BSES Yamuna (BYPL) East and Central Delhi BYPL application steps, builder-floor and shared roof solutions
Tata Power-DDL (TPDDL) North and North-West Delhi TPDDL portal steps, typical timelines, inspection support
NDMC New Delhi Municipal Council area NDMC solar registration process, government and commercial buildings

Boundaries are by colony, not just by zone, so let the buyer confirm their DISCOM from their bill. Your landing page can show "Check the name on top of your electricity bill" with a picture of each bill format.

Run one campaign per DISCOM cluster

Instead of one "Delhi" campaign, create ad sets or ad groups for each DISCOM area, each with:

  • Locality targeting by pin codes in that area
  • A headline naming the DISCOM ("Rooftop solar for BSES Rajdhani homes")
  • A landing page section explaining that DISCOM's net metering steps
  • Photos and videos from installations in that part of Delhi, if you have them

This makes the ad more relevant, lifts click-through and gives your sales team a ready-made opening line.

Subsidy messaging: accurate beats exciting

Central subsidy under PM Surya Ghar

As of November 2026, PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana gives residential consumers a central subsidy of ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third kW, with a cap of ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above. Applications go through pmsuryaghar.gov.in and use an empanelled vendor. Modules must meet the scheme's domestic content (ALMM) rules.

Delhi's own state support

Delhi offers state support on top of the central subsidy through its solar policy and the Delhi Solar Portal (solar.delhi.gov.in). In September 2026, the Delhi Cabinet approved enhanced rooftop solar support aimed at domestic households consuming up to about 400 units a month, including a zero-upfront-cost route, with a target reported at 2.30 lakh rooftops by March 2027. Installer blogs online still quote different figures and processes for it.

What this means for your ads:

  • Never put an unverified state amount in an ad. Say "central subsidy up to ₹78,000 plus Delhi state support, as per current policy" and explain details on the call.
  • Never use "free solar panels" wording. Even where a zero-upfront route exists, eligibility, sizes and conditions apply.
  • Update your landing page the day the official guidelines change, and date it ("Updated November 2026").

This is general information, not financial advice; buyers should confirm amounts and eligibility on the official portals.

The Delhi rooftop reality check

Delhi buyers often have less roof than they think. Put a short "Will solar fit my roof?" section on your landing page:

  • Roughly 80–100 sq ft of shadow-free roof per kW is a common rule of thumb
  • Elevated structures can lift panels above water tanks and allow use of the terrace below
  • Neighbouring buildings can cause shading; a site survey is essential
  • On builder floors, confirm who owns the roof and get written consent where needed

Honest roof guidance filters out leads who cannot install and builds trust with those who can.

Channels that work for a solar company in Delhi

  1. Google Search for high-intent terms like "rooftop solar Delhi", "solar installer near me" and "BSES net metering". See our Google Ads keyword guide for solar installers.
  2. Meta lead ads and WhatsApp ads by DISCOM cluster, with a short qualifier form: DISCOM, monthly bill, roof type, own or rented.
  3. Google Business Profile with real installation photos and reviews from Delhi customers.
  4. Installation reels filmed on Delhi rooftops, showing the elevated structure, wiring and meter.
  5. RWA and market association outreach for societies and shops.

Qualify before the site visit

Delhi site visits are expensive in time and traffic. Ask on WhatsApp first:

  • Which DISCOM is on your bill?
  • Average monthly bill in summer?
  • Independent house, builder floor or society?
  • Do you own the roof, and is it shadow-free?
  • Is the connection in your name?

Leads that answer these are far more likely to convert.

Frequently asked questions

How much subsidy can a Delhi home get for rooftop solar?

The central PM Surya Ghar subsidy is ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third kW, capped at ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above, as of November 2026. Delhi also offers state support under its solar policy, and the Cabinet approved enhanced support in September 2026. Check pmsuryaghar.gov.in and solar.delhi.gov.in for the current amounts and eligibility.

Does net metering work the same with BSES, Tata Power-DDL and NDMC?

The rules come from the Delhi Electricity Regulatory Commission, so the basics are the same, but each DISCOM has its own application portal, documents, inspection and meter installation timelines. A Delhi installer should know all of them and explain the steps for the buyer's specific DISCOM.

Which areas of Delhi are best for rooftop solar ads?

Independent houses, builder floors with roof rights and group housing societies with large common roofs respond best. Target by locality and pin-code clusters within each DISCOM area, for example Dwarka, Janakpuri, Rohini, Pitampura, Mayur Vihar or South Delhi colonies, rather than running one city-wide campaign.
