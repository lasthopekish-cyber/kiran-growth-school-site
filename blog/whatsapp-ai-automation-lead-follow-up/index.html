WhatsApp and AI Automation for Lead Follow-up: A Setup Guide for Solar and Real Estate Teams

How to connect Meta, Google and website leads to WhatsApp and a simple CRM so every enquiry gets an instant reply, gets routed to the right salesperson and is followed up on time.

By Kiran Growth School team·11 November 2026·3 min read
Quick answer

Send every new lead an instant WhatsApp message, qualify it with a few questions (optionally with an AI assistant), route it to the right salesperson, and run a day 1, 3, 7 and 14 follow-up sequence. Use the official WhatsApp Business Platform for automation at scale.

Most businesses don't have a lead problem; they have a follow-up problem. A homeowner fills a solar form at 9 pm, nobody replies until 11 am, and by then another installer has already called. A property buyer enquires on three projects; the first developer to send the brochure and a visit slot usually gets the visit.

Automation fixes this without hiring more people.

The basic flow

Ad lead (Meta / Google / website) → instant WhatsApp message → qualifying questions (bot or AI assistant) → lead scored and tagged → assigned to the right salesperson (+ notification) → follow-up sequence until booked or closed → status sent back to ad platforms

Step 1: Capture every lead in one place

Leads come from Meta lead forms, Google lead forms, website forms, WhatsApp click ads and calls. Connect them all to one sheet or CRM, with these fields:

  • Name, phone, city or area
  • Source (Meta, Google, website, referral) and campaign or ad name
  • Answers to qualifying questions
  • Assigned salesperson, status and next follow-up date

Tools like Meta's CRM integrations, Zapier, Make, Pabbly or a custom webhook can move leads automatically.

Step 2: Reply within a minute on WhatsApp

Use the WhatsApp Business Platform (via Meta or an approved provider) to send an approved template message the moment a lead arrives:

Hi Ramesh, thanks for your interest in rooftop solar. I'm sharing a 40-second video on how the PM Surya Ghar subsidy works. Could you send a photo of your latest electricity bill so we can suggest the right system size?

For real estate:

Hi Neha, thanks for enquiring about [Project]. Here's the brochure and location pin. Would Saturday 11 am or Sunday 4 pm suit you for a site visit?

Step 3: Qualify (with or without AI)

A simple button-based bot can ask three or four questions. An AI assistant goes further: it can answer common questions in Hindi or English, read the bill photo the customer sends, and summarise the conversation for your salesperson.

Keep AI on a short leash:

  • Give it approved facts only (prices, subsidy rules, project details)
  • Never let it promise discounts, approvals or returns
  • Hand over to a human as soon as the lead is hot or asks something unusual

Step 4: Route to the right person

Assign leads automatically by city, project, language or lead score, and notify the salesperson on WhatsApp or by app notification. Round-robin works for equal teams; score-based routing sends hot leads to your best closer.

Step 5: Follow up until there's an answer

A simple sequence:

Day Message
0 Instant reply + qualifying questions
1 Short video (subsidy explainer or project walkthrough)
3 Customer story or site/installation photos
7 Offer: free survey slot or weekend site visit with pickup
14 "Should I close your enquiry?" check-in

Stop the sequence automatically when the lead replies or books.

Step 6: Close the loop with the ad platforms

Update status in your sheet or CRM (contacted, survey/visit booked, won). Send these stages back to Meta and Google as conversions. This teaches the platforms which people actually buy, so your ads find more of them. We cover this in Meta Ads for solar companies.

Rules that keep your number safe

  • Only message people who opted in (a form submission with clear consent counts)
  • Use approved templates for business-initiated messages
  • Give an easy way to opt out
  • Don't buy contact lists

What owners should see every day

A daily summary on WhatsApp: new leads, leads contacted within 5 minutes, surveys or visits booked, and deals won. If "contacted within 5 minutes" is low, that's where money is leaking.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API for automation?

For automatic messages to new leads at scale, yes. Use the WhatsApp Business Platform through Meta or an approved provider. The free WhatsApp Business app is fine for manual chats and quick replies.

Can I send bulk WhatsApp messages to old leads?

Only to people who have opted in, using approved message templates. Spammy blasts can get your number restricted.

What CRM should a small solar or real estate business use?

Start with a well-structured Google Sheet. When you have several salespeople and hundreds of leads a month, move to a CRM that integrates with WhatsApp and your ad platforms.
