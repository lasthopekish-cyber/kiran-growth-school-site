Quick answer

Good video editing services in Delhi NCR turn raw phone footage into reels that stop the scroll in the first two seconds, carry burned-in captions for people watching on mute, have clean audio, and end with one clear action such as "WhatsApp us". Ask any editor for 9:16 exports at 1080x1920, a hook in the first frame, safe-zone text placement and a fixed turnaround. The footage is usually fine. What separates a reel that brings enquiries from one that gets 200 views is the edit. If you are comparing video editing services in Delhi NCR, this guide explains what a good business edit actually includes, so you can judge an editor's work and brief them properly.

Why the edit matters more than the camera

People scroll fast. On Instagram, Facebook and YouTube Shorts, viewers decide in the first second or two whether to keep watching. A sharp edit does four things a raw clip cannot:

  1. Earns attention with a hook in the first frame
  2. Holds it by cutting every pause, "umm" and dead second
  3. Works on mute, because many people watch without sound in metros, offices and Delhi Metro coaches
  4. Asks for one action, such as "WhatsApp us" or "Book a site visit"

A phone video edited well will usually do better than an expensive shoot edited badly.

What good video editing services in Delhi NCR should include

Use this as a checklist when you review an editor's sample work or your own monthly reels.

Element What good looks like Common mistake
Hook (first 1 to 2 seconds) A bold claim, question, result or movement on screen, plus matching text Logo animation or "Hi guys, welcome"
Pacing Cuts every 1 to 3 seconds, pauses removed, jump cuts used on purpose Long static shots, slow intros
Captions Burned-in, large, high contrast, 3 to 6 words per line, correct Hindi and English spelling Auto-captions with errors, tiny white text on white walls
Audio Voice levelled, background noise reduced, music low under speech Music louder than voice, traffic noise, echo
Safe zones Key text kept away from the top, bottom and right edge where app buttons sit Price or phone number hidden under the caption bar
Branding Logo small or only at the end, brand colours in text Watermark covering the product
Call to action One clear step on screen and in the caption Three different CTAs, or none
Export 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, correct frame rate, no black bars Horizontal video cropped badly, blurry exports

Hooks that work for local businesses

  • Result first: show the finished cake, the installed rooftop or the renovated room, then explain how
  • Question: "Planning a 3 kW solar system in Noida? Watch this first."
  • Price or offer (only if true): "What ₹25,000 gets you in a website"
  • Myth-busting: "No, rooftop solar does not leak in the monsoon if it is installed right"

Captions in Hindi, English or Hinglish

Delhi NCR audiences are mixed. A coaching centre in Laxmi Nagar might do best with Hinglish captions; a Gurugram SaaS company with English. Match the language people actually speak in the video, keep spellings consistent, and proofread every caption. Errors look careless and cost trust.

How to brief an editor (and get fewer revisions)

A clear brief saves days. Send these with your footage:

  • [ ] Goal: reach, enquiries, or a specific offer
  • [ ] Audience: who should watch, and in which language
  • [ ] Hook idea: the first line or shot you want, or ask the editor to suggest three
  • [ ] Key message: one sentence, not five
  • [ ] CTA: the exact action and the phone or WhatsApp number to show
  • [ ] Brand kit: logo, colours, fonts
  • [ ] Reference reels: two or three you like, and why
  • [ ] Deadline and number of revision rounds

For shooting, a simple rule helps: vertical, good window light, a clip-on mic, and three seconds of silence before and after each take.

Turnaround, pricing and what to ask

Prices for reel editing in Delhi NCR vary widely depending on length, motion graphics, the number of versions and whether scripting or shooting is included, so compare scope rather than per-reel price alone. Ask every editor:

  1. Will I get the project files or only the final export?
  2. How many revision rounds are included?
  3. What is the turnaround per reel, and for a batch of 8 to 12 a month?
  4. Do you write hooks and captions, or only cut?
  5. Can you deliver ad variations and cut-downs?
  6. Is licensed music used, or do you add sound in-app?

Music matters: use licensed tracks or the platform's own audio library, especially for ads, to avoid takedowns or muted videos.

Frequently asked questions

What should business reel editing include?

A strong hook in the first one or two seconds, tight cuts that remove pauses, burned-in captions, clean and levelled audio, brand colours and logo used lightly, text kept inside the safe zone, and a clear call to action at the end. Exports should be 9:16 at 1080x1920.

How long should a business reel be?

Instagram allows reels up to 3 minutes as of November 2026, but most business reels work best between 15 and 45 seconds. Ad edits are often shorter. Test two or three lengths and keep the one with better watch time and enquiries.

Do I need professional shooting before editing?

Not always. Clear phone footage shot vertically in good light, with a clip-on mic, is enough for most business reels. Good editing fixes pacing, captions and sound; it cannot fully fix shaky, dark or noisy footage, so a short shooting brief helps.
