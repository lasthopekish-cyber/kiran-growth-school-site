The High-Converting Website Checklist for Solar and Real Estate Businesses (SEO + AI Search Ready)

A 25-point checklist for solar company and real estate websites: speed, WhatsApp CTAs, local SEO pages, schema markup and content that Google, ChatGPT and Claude can understand and cite.

By Kiran Growth School team·11 November 2026·3 min read
Quick answer

A website that converts ad traffic and ranks in search needs a fast mobile first screen with a WhatsApp button, one page per service and city, real proof, clear FAQ answers, schema markup, and crawlable text that AI search tools can quote.

Your website is where every ad click, Google search and AI recommendation lands. If it's slow, vague or hard to contact you from, you pay for traffic that never becomes a lead. Here's the checklist we use for solar and real estate sites.

1. The first screen (above the fold)

  1. A clear headline that says what you do and where: "Rooftop solar installer in Ghaziabad" or "3 BHK homes on Kolar Road, Bhopal".
  2. One key number: subsidy up to ₹78,000, starting price, or possession date.
  3. A WhatsApp button and a call button, both visible on mobile without scrolling.
  4. A trust signal: Google rating, years in business, RERA number, or "registered PM Surya Ghar vendor" (only if true).

2. Speed and mobile

  1. Main content loads in about 2.5 seconds or less on a 4G phone.
  2. Images are compressed and sized for mobile (WebP or AVIF).
  3. No heavy sliders, autoplay background videos or pop-ups on load.
  4. Text is readable without zooming; buttons are big enough to tap.

3. Structure for SEO

  1. One page per service (rooftop solar, commercial solar, solar pumps; or each project).
  2. One page per city you genuinely serve, with real local details: DISCOM, areas served, local installations or projects. Avoid copy-paste pages with only the city name changed.
  3. Clean URLs (/locations/gurugram/, /services/meta-ads/).
  4. Unique title tags and meta descriptions on every page.
  5. Internal links between services, cities and blog posts.
  6. An XML sitemap submitted to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

4. Content that answers questions

  1. An FAQ on every key page with short, direct answers.
  2. A blog that answers real customer questions: subsidy steps, net metering, RERA, home loans, maintenance.
  3. Specific facts with sources, not vague claims.
  4. Content in the language your buyers use. A Hindi version of key pages can help in MP and UP.

5. Ready for AI search (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews)

AI assistants increasingly answer questions like "best solar installer in Bhopal" or "how to get PM Surya Ghar subsidy". To be understood and cited:

  1. Allow AI crawlers in robots.txt (for example GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot) unless you have a reason to block them.
  2. Add structured data (Organization, LocalBusiness or ProfessionalService, Service, FAQPage, Article, BreadcrumbList).
  3. Publish an llms.txt file that summarises who you are and links to key pages. It's a newer convention, not a guarantee, but it's cheap to add.
  4. Keep important information in plain HTML text, not only inside images or PDFs.
  5. Be consistent: the same business name, phone and address on your site, Google Business Profile and directories.

6. Proof and conversion

  1. Real photos: installations, sites, sample flats, team. Avoid only stock photos.
  2. Tracking: GA4, Google Ads conversions, Meta Pixel and Conversions API, plus WhatsApp and call click events.

Special notes for solar sites

  • A bill-to-system-size calculator that ends in a WhatsApp message is one of the best lead tools.
  • Explain the PM Surya Ghar process step by step and link to the official portal.
  • Show warranty, panel and inverter brands, and after-sales support.

Special notes for real estate sites

  • RERA number near the top of every project page (see our RERA advertising guide).
  • Floor plans and price sheets behind a WhatsApp or form gate.
  • Location map with real drive times.
  • Construction updates with dates.

Don't forget the Google Business Profile

For local searches, the Maps listing often gets more clicks than the website. Complete every field, add photos weekly, reply to reviews and link to the right city page.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my website to show up in ChatGPT or Claude answers?

Make sure AI crawlers are allowed in robots.txt, publish clear factual pages about your services and locations, answer common questions directly, add structured data, and get mentioned on other trusted sites. Being indexed by Bing also helps, as several AI search tools use web search results.

Do I need separate pages for each city?

If you genuinely serve several cities, yes, but each page should have real local information (areas served, local rules, local projects or installations), not just the city name swapped.

How fast should my landing page load?

Aim for the main content to appear within about 2.5 seconds on a 4G phone, which is Google's 'good' threshold for Largest Contentful Paint.
