Meta Ads for Solar Companies in India: The Campaign Setup That Brings Real Site Surveys

How to structure Facebook and Instagram ads for rooftop solar: objectives, lead forms vs WhatsApp, creatives, targeting and tracking that bring qualified solar leads.

By Kiran Growth School team·11 November 2026·4 min read
Quick answer

For solar, run a Leads campaign with a qualifying instant form and a click-to-WhatsApp campaign side by side, lead with bill savings and the PM Surya Ghar subsidy, test video creatives weekly, and send real lead quality back to Meta through the Conversions API.

Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) are the fastest way for a solar installer to create demand in a city. People don't wake up searching for solar panels, but they do open Instagram every day, and a good ad can make them think about their ₹3,000 electricity bill.

The problem is that solar on Meta can produce a lot of junk leads if it's set up badly. Here is the structure we recommend.

Pick the right campaign objective

Use the Leads objective. Inside it you have two good options:

  • Instant forms: the form opens inside Facebook or Instagram. Highest volume, but quality depends on your questions.
  • Click-to-WhatsApp (messaging): the ad opens a WhatsApp chat with your business. Fewer leads, but the conversation is usually warmer.

We usually run both for the first month and then shift budget to whichever gives the lower cost per qualified lead for that installer and city.

Avoid Traffic or Engagement objectives for lead generation. They optimise for clicks and likes, not enquiries.

Write a form that filters

Choose the Higher intent form type, which adds a review screen before submission. Then ask:

  1. What is your average monthly electricity bill? (Below ₹1,500 / ₹1,500 to ₹3,000 / ₹3,000 to ₹6,000 / Above ₹6,000)
  2. Do you own the house? (Yes / No, I rent)
  3. Type of property (Independent house / Builder floor / Society flat / Shop or office)
  4. Your city or area

Each extra question lowers volume a little but raises quality a lot. Renters in society flats, for example, usually can't install, so it's better to know before you call.

Lead with money, not hardware

Homeowners don't buy panels; they buy a lower bill. Strong angles for solar ads in India:

  • Subsidy angle: "Government subsidy up to ₹78,000 on home rooftop solar. Check your eligibility in 2 minutes."
  • Bill angle: "Paying ₹4,000 a month for electricity? See what a 3 kW system can save."
  • Proof angle: Real customer, real roof, real bill before and after (with permission).
  • Process angle: "We handle PM Surya Ghar registration, DISCOM approval and net metering for you."

Keep claims accurate. Never promise "free solar", and don't make the ad look like an official government notice.

Creatives that work

Test three to five creatives at a time, and refresh every two to three weeks:

  • Short explainer reel (20 to 40 seconds): hook, subsidy amount, savings, call to action.
  • Founder or engineer talking to camera: builds trust in local markets like Rewa, Satna and Bhopal, ideally in Hindi.
  • Installation timelapse: shows you are a real company with real teams.
  • AI avatar explainer: quick to update when subsidy rules or prices change. See AI avatar videos for solar and real estate.
  • Carousel: "1 kW vs 2 kW vs 3 kW: subsidy and savings" works well for retargeting.

Targeting: broad, but local

Target your service radius (for example, 20 to 30 km around your office or warehouse), adults 25+, and otherwise keep targeting broad. Meta's delivery system usually finds buyers better than narrow interest stacks when the creative and form do the filtering.

Exclude areas you can't serve, and split campaigns by city or DISCOM when the message changes. A Ghaziabad homeowner (PVVNL) and a Gurugram homeowner (DHBVN) go through different net-metering offices, so the details in your follow-up should differ.

Tracking: send quality back to Meta

This is where most solar campaigns fail. If Meta only sees "lead submitted", it will find more people who submit forms, including people who never pick up.

  • Install the Meta Pixel and Conversions API on your website.
  • Mark leads in your CRM or sheet as contacted, survey booked and installed.
  • Send those stages back to Meta as conversion events (the CRM integration or Conversions API for CRM).

Over a few weeks, Meta learns which kind of person actually books a survey and finds more of them.

Follow-up decides the result

Even a perfect campaign fails if leads wait. Reply on WhatsApp within five minutes, ideally automatically, with a subsidy explainer and a request for their latest bill photo. Then call. Our guide to WhatsApp and AI automation shows how.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good budget to start Meta Ads for solar?

Many local installers start testing with ₹500 to ₹1,500 a day per city. Run for at least two weeks before judging, and judge by cost per site survey, not cost per lead.

Should solar ads target by age and income?

Keep targeting broad within your service area and let the creative and form questions filter. Over-narrow targeting usually raises costs without improving quality.

Do video ads work better than images for solar?

Usually yes for cold audiences, because a short video can explain savings and the subsidy. Keep images and carousels for retargeting and offers.
