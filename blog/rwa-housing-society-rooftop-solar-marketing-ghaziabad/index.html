How Solar Installers Can Sell Rooftop Solar to RWAs and Housing Societies in Ghaziabad

A practical guide for solar installers to market rooftop solar to RWAs and group housing societies in Ghaziabad and Delhi NCR, using the PM Surya Ghar GHS/RWA subsidy.

By Kiran Growth School team·12 November 2026·6 min read
Quick answer

Housing societies buy solar as a committee, not as individuals, so installers win by giving the RWA a clear proposal, a one-page subsidy explainer and proof for the general body meeting. Under PM Surya Ghar, group housing societies and RWAs can get central assistance of ₹18,000 per kW for common facilities, up to 500 kW (as of November 2026), which makes common-area solar an easy first conversation.

Ghaziabad has hundreds of group housing societies across Indirapuram, Vaishali, Raj Nagar Extension, Crossings Republik and Siddharth Vihar. Most of them pay large bills every month for lifts, pumps, corridor lights, clubhouses and basement parking. That makes housing societies one of the best, and most overlooked, segments for rooftop solar installers in Delhi NCR.

The catch is that a society does not buy like a homeowner. One flat owner can say yes in a week. A society needs a committee discussion, three quotes, a general body meeting and sometimes a second meeting. This guide shows how to market to that buying process.

Why RWAs are a strong segment right now

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana has a separate provision for group housing societies (GHS) and resident welfare associations (RWAs). As of November 2026, according to the scheme details published on official state solar portals:

  • ₹18,000 per kW central assistance for common facilities, including EV charging
  • Up to 500 kW per society, calculated at 3 kW per house
  • The 500 kW limit includes individual rooftop systems that residents install in the same society

The scheme itself is moving fast. MNRE said on 4 August 2026 that rooftop solar had reached more than 50 lakh households, and in early November 2026 the government reported a pace of roughly one lakh installations every six days. Awareness is high, so committees are more open to the conversation than they were two years ago.

Always check the latest figures on pmsuryaghar.gov.in before you put numbers in an ad or proposal. For individual homes, the central subsidy is up to ₹78,000 (as of November 2026), and Uttar Pradesh has offered a state top-up for residential consumers; confirm the current status with UPNEDA before using it in marketing. Never call it "free solar panels".

Who you are really selling to

Stakeholder What they care about What to give them
RWA president / secretary Savings, no controversy, smooth process One-page proposal with savings range and timeline
Treasurer Cost, subsidy, payback, maintenance fund Simple cost table, subsidy explainer, AMC terms
Maintenance / facility manager Roof load, waterproofing, downtime Site survey report, structure drawings, safety plan
Residents at the GBM "Will my maintenance charges come down?" Short video and a common-area bill comparison
Builder (if not handed over) Roof rights, warranty issues NOC format and a clear scope of work

Your ads and content should speak to the committee first. Residents come later, through the material the committee shares.

Marketing channels that work for societies

1. Meta Ads aimed at committee members

You cannot target "RWA secretaries" directly, but you can reach the right people with location targeting around specific societies and creatives that call out the role. A headline like "RWA committee in Indirapuram? See what common-area solar can save" filters the audience on its own.

Good formats:

  • A 30-45 second reel explaining the GHS/RWA subsidy in simple Hindi or Hinglish
  • A carousel showing the process: survey, proposal, GBM approval, installation, net meter, subsidy
  • A lead form that asks for society name, number of flats and average common-area bill

See our Meta Ads service for how we set up lead forms and WhatsApp follow-up for solar companies.

2. Google Search for high-intent queries

Committees research before they call. Bid on searches such as "solar for housing society Ghaziabad", "RWA rooftop solar subsidy" and "society solar installer near me". Send them to a landing page built for societies, not the same page you use for homeowners.

3. A society-specific landing page

The page should include:

  • The GHS/RWA subsidy explained in two lines, with the "as of" date
  • A savings calculator that uses the common-area bill
  • A downloadable proposal template the secretary can forward on WhatsApp
  • Photos of your real installations, with permission (no stock photos of foreign rooftops)
  • A clear WhatsApp button

If you don't have one yet, our website development team can build a fast landing page in about 48 hours.

4. WhatsApp kits for the general body meeting

The real sale happens in the GBM. Give the committee a ready "GBM kit":

  • A 60-second explainer video (an AI avatar video in Hindi works well and is easy to update when numbers change)
  • A one-page PDF with cost, subsidy, savings range and maintenance terms
  • A short FAQ that answers roof leaks, monsoon output, cleaning and who owns the system

5. Society events and offline touchpoints

Solar camps on a Sunday morning at the clubhouse, a stall at the society's Diwali or Holi event, or a talk during an RWA federation meeting all build trust. Put a QR code on every standee that opens your WhatsApp chat.

Proposal checklist for RWAs

  • [ ] Site survey with roof area, shadow analysis and structure check
  • [ ] Recommended capacity and why (based on common-area load, not guesswork)
  • [ ] Total cost, central assistance and net cost to the society
  • [ ] Savings shown as a range, with assumptions stated
  • [ ] Net metering steps with PVVNL (or the relevant DISCOM)
  • [ ] Waterproofing and roof-safety plan
  • [ ] Warranty, AMC and cleaning schedule
  • [ ] Timeline from GBM approval to commissioning
  • [ ] Contact person for resident questions

Common objections and how to answer them

"Our roof will leak." Show your mounting method and waterproofing process, and offer a written commitment on roof repair.

"Output drops in monsoon and winter smog." Agree, and show annual generation instead of a single sunny month.

"The next committee may not support it." Keep everything documented: GBM resolution, proposal, warranty. Offer an annual performance report the committee can share with residents.

"Is this the same as individual flat solar?" No. Common-area solar reduces the society's bill. Individual flat solar needs roof space and separate approvals, and both count within the 500 kW society limit.

Beyond Ghaziabad

The same approach works for societies in Noida, Gurugram, Dwarka and across Delhi, though the DISCOM and state rules change. Delhi has BSES and Tata Power-DDL, Gurugram has DHBVN. For a city-wise view, see our Delhi NCR page and Ghaziabad page. For homeowner campaigns, read our PM Surya Ghar lead generation guide.

Results vary by society size, roof, budget and how quickly you follow up, so start small and measure qualified meetings, not just leads.

Frequently asked questions

What subsidy can an RWA or housing society get under PM Surya Ghar?

As of November 2026, the central assistance for group housing societies and RWAs is ₹18,000 per kW for common facilities, including EV charging, up to 500 kW at 3 kW per house. The limit includes individual rooftop plants residents install in the same society. Always confirm on pmsuryaghar.gov.in before quoting.

Who decides on solar in a housing society?

Usually the managing committee shortlists vendors and the general body approves the spend. Your marketing has to convince the committee first and then help them convince residents.

Which DISCOM handles net metering in Ghaziabad?

Ghaziabad is served by Paschimanchal Vidyut Vitran Nigam Limited (PVVNL). Net metering and subsidy steps go through the PM Surya Ghar portal and the DISCOM, so check PVVNL's current process for group housing connections.
