Real Estate Lead Generation in Gurugram: A Playbook for Builders and Channel Partners

How builders, brokers and channel partners in Gurugram can generate exclusive property leads with Meta Ads, Google Ads, project microsites and fast WhatsApp follow-up.

By Kiran Growth School team·11 November 2026·3 min read
Quick answer

In Gurugram, sell the corridor as much as the project, run separate campaigns per project and buyer type, show the HRERA registration on every ad and page, and judge campaigns by site visits booked rather than leads.

Gurugram is one of the most competitive real estate advertising markets in India. New launches compete on Dwarka Expressway, Golf Course Extension Road, Sohna Road, Southern Peripheral Road and New Gurugram, and buyers see dozens of property ads every week.

That means generic ads ("Luxury 3 & 4 BHK, Book Now!") rarely work. Here is what does.

Sell the corridor, then the project

Most Gurugram buyers first decide where they want to live and only then which project. Your ads and landing pages should answer corridor-level questions:

  • How far is it from Cyber City, the airport or the Delhi border?
  • What's the status of the expressway, metro or upcoming social infrastructure?
  • What are prices like compared with the next corridor?

A short reel titled "Dwarka Expressway vs Golf Course Extension: which suits you?" often generates more qualified interest than a straight project ad, because it helps the buyer decide.

Separate campaigns by project and buyer

Don't mix everything into one campaign. At minimum, separate:

  • Each project (different price, location and possession date)
  • End users vs investors (different messages: lifestyle and schools vs appreciation and rental)
  • NRI buyers (different timezone, different concerns, different targeting)

Separate campaigns make it clear which project is actually selling and stop one project's budget being spent on another's audience.

Lead forms that respect sales time

Use Meta's higher-intent instant forms with three to four questions:

  1. Budget range
  2. Preferred configuration (2, 3 or 4 BHK)
  3. Buying timeline (within 3 months / 3 to 6 months / just exploring)
  4. Purpose (self use / investment)

Fewer leads, but your sales team spends time on people who can actually buy.

Google Search for active buyers

Google Search captures buyers searching for exact terms: "3 BHK Dwarka Expressway ready to move", "new launch Sector 89 Gurugram", "luxury flats Golf Course Extension". Build tight ad groups by corridor and configuration, and send each to a matching page.

Be careful with competitor and builder brand keywords. Bidding on another developer's trademarked name in ad text can get ads disapproved; check Google's trademark policy before using them.

Show HRERA details everywhere

Haryana RERA (HRERA Gurugram for this district) expects registered projects to display their registration number in advertisements. Put the RERA number and website on every creative, landing page and brochure. It's a legal requirement and also builds trust. Read our full guide to RERA-compliant real estate ads.

Build a microsite for each project

A project microsite works better than sending traffic to a portal listing or a generic builder site. It should include:

  • RERA number, location map and connectivity
  • Configuration, carpet area and price on request (or starting price, if you can publish it)
  • Floor plans behind a WhatsApp or form gate
  • Walkthrough video and construction updates
  • Site-visit booking with date and time slots

Video sells space

Buyers want to feel the space before visiting. Use:

  • Walkthrough reels from renders or the sample flat
  • Corridor explainers with maps and drive times
  • Short founder or sales-head videos answering common questions
  • AI avatar videos for quick updates on offers and possession (see AI avatar videos)

Measure site visits, not leads

In Gurugram, a ₹300 lead that never visits is worse than a ₹1,200 lead that books a visit. Track:

  • Cost per lead
  • Contact rate (how many picked up)
  • Cost per site visit
  • Cost per booking

Send site visit done back to Meta and Google as a conversion so the platforms learn which buyers actually show up.

Speed wins

Buyers fill forms on several projects the same evening. Reply on WhatsApp within minutes with the brochure, location pin and two visit slots. Automation makes this possible even at 11 pm. See WhatsApp and AI automation for lead follow-up.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best platform for real estate leads in Gurugram?

Meta Ads usually give the most leads for launches and mid-premium projects, while Google Search brings fewer but higher-intent buyers searching for specific corridors and configurations. Most projects need both.

Can channel partners run Facebook ads for a builder's project?

Yes, if the builder allows it and the creatives, prices and RERA details are accurate and approved. Many developers share an approved creative kit with partners.

Why are my property leads not picking up the phone?

Usually because follow-up is slow, the form was too easy, or the lead was shared. Add qualifying questions, reply on WhatsApp in minutes and use your own ads instead of shared portal leads.
