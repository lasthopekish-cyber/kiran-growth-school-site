How to Choose a Performance Marketing Agency in Delhi NCR for Your Solar or Real Estate Business

Twelve questions to ask before hiring a performance marketing agency in Delhi, Gurugram, Ghaziabad or Noida, plus red flags, pricing models and what a good first month looks like.

By Kiran Growth School team·11 November 2026·3 min read
Quick answer

Choose an agency that understands your industry, works inside your own ad accounts, tracks cost per qualified lead and site visit (not just clicks), sets up WhatsApp follow-up, and reports weekly in plain language. Avoid guaranteed results and agencies that keep your accounts.

Delhi NCR has hundreds of digital marketing agencies. Many are good at posting on Instagram; far fewer can reliably turn ad spend into site surveys, site visits and sales for a solar installer or a real estate developer. Here's how to tell the difference.

Performance marketing vs "digital marketing"

Performance marketing means you pay for, and judge by, measurable outcomes: leads, qualified leads, calls, visits and sales. A performance agency should talk about cost per lead and cost per site visit in the first meeting, not followers and impressions.

12 questions to ask before you sign

  1. Have you worked with solar or real estate businesses? Ask what worked, what didn't, and what they learned.
  2. Will the ad accounts be in my name? They should be. Your data and history must stay with you.
  3. What will you track? Look for leads, qualified leads, calls, WhatsApp chats, site visits or surveys, and sales.
  4. How do you qualify leads? Good answers mention form questions, WhatsApp bots and sending lead quality back to Meta and Google.
  5. Who makes the creatives, and how often? Expect new creatives every few weeks, including video.
  6. How do you handle follow-up? An agency that ignores what happens after the lead is only doing half the job.
  7. What does the landing page look like? Sending all traffic to a generic home page is a red flag.
  8. How do you handle compliance? For real estate: RERA numbers on every ad. For solar: accurate subsidy claims.
  9. How often do you report, and what's in it? Weekly, in plain language, with what changed and what's next.
  10. Who will actually work on my account? Meet them.
  11. What's the minimum commitment and notice period?
  12. What do you need from me? Good agencies ask for photos, approvals, sales feedback and quick replies.

Red flags

  • Guaranteed leads, rankings or ROI. Nobody can honestly guarantee results; they depend on your offer, market and budget.
  • Agency-owned ad accounts that you can't access or take with you.
  • Reports full of reach and impressions but no cost per lead or lead quality.
  • One campaign for all of Delhi NCR, ignoring the differences between Delhi, Gurugram (Haryana), and Ghaziabad and Noida (UP).
  • No questions about your sales process.

Whatever the model, ad spend should be paid directly to Meta or Google from your own account.

What a good first month looks like

  • Week 1: audit, tracking setup (Pixel, Conversions API, GA4, call and WhatsApp tracking), offer and messaging plan
  • Week 2: landing pages, creatives, campaign launch
  • Week 3: first optimisation, WhatsApp automation live, sales feedback loop
  • Week 4: first monthly review with clear data and next steps

Why industry focus matters

Solar buyers worry about subsidy, savings and trust; property buyers worry about location, price, possession and RERA. An agency that knows these buyer journeys writes better ads, builds better landing pages and sets up better follow-up from day one.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a performance marketing agency charge in Delhi NCR?

Most agencies charge a fixed monthly fee, a percentage of ad spend, or a mix, plus your ad budget paid directly to Meta or Google. Websites, videos and automation are usually quoted separately. Compare what is included, not just the fee.

What results should I expect in the first month?

The first month is for tracking, landing pages, creatives and testing. Expect early leads and clear data on what works, and expect cost per lead to improve over the following one to three months.

Should I hire a general agency or one that specialises in my industry?

For solar and real estate, specialists usually ramp up faster because they already know the buyer journey, compliance rules (like RERA or subsidy claims) and the follow-up process that turns leads into visits.
