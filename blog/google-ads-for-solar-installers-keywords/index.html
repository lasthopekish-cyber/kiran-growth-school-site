Google Ads for Solar Installers: Keywords, Negatives and Campaign Structure for 2026

A ready-to-use Google Ads plan for rooftop solar companies in India, with keyword groups, negative keywords, ad copy ideas, call tracking and landing page tips.

By Kiran Growth School team·11 November 2026·3 min read
Quick answer

Build separate Search ad groups for subsidy, price, local and commercial intent, add a long negative keyword list from day one, track calls and WhatsApp clicks as conversions, and send each ad group to a matching landing page.

Google Ads catches homeowners at the moment they are actively looking for solar. Volume is smaller than Meta, but intent is higher. For many installers, Google Search brings the leads that convert fastest.

Here is a practical setup you can use for any city in Delhi NCR or Madhya Pradesh.

Campaign structure

Keep it simple: one Search campaign per city or service area, with these ad groups.

Ad group Example keywords Landing page
Local installer solar company in Gurugram, solar installer near me, rooftop solar Ghaziabad City page with reviews and WhatsApp button
Subsidy PM Surya Ghar vendor, solar subsidy apply, rooftop solar subsidy Delhi Subsidy explainer with eligibility check
Price 3kW solar system price, on grid solar cost, 5kW solar price Price and sizing page with calculator
Commercial commercial rooftop solar, solar for factory, solar EPC company Commercial page with case studies
Brand your company name Home page

Use phrase match and exact match at the start. Broad match can work later, once you have conversion data and Smart Bidding is learning from real leads.

Negative keywords to add on day one

Solar searches attract a lot of people who will never buy. Start with a shared negative list:

  • free, free panel, muft panel, yojana form pdf, apply online government
  • job, jobs, salary, vacancy, internship, course, training
  • franchise, dealership, distributor, wholesale, manufacturer
  • DIY, how to make, project, ppt, essay, wikipedia
  • second hand, used, scrap
  • share price, stock, IPO

Careful with "PM Surya Ghar" keywords: many searchers want the government portal, not a vendor. Use them, but write ads that clearly say you are a registered vendor who handles the process, and watch the search terms closely.

Ad copy that earns the click

Responsive Search Ads let you add up to 15 headlines. Mix these themes:

  • Subsidy: "Subsidy up to ₹78,000 on Home Solar", "We Handle PM Surya Ghar Paperwork"
  • Local: "Rooftop Solar Installer in Ghaziabad", "Serving Bhopal and Nearby Towns"
  • Proof: "500+ Rooftops Installed" (only if true), "Rated on Google by Real Customers"
  • Offer: "Free Site Survey", "Get a Quote on WhatsApp"

Add call assets, location assets (linked to your Google Business Profile), sitelinks (Subsidy, Price, Commercial, Reviews) and structured snippets (system sizes, services).

Track what actually matters

Set up conversions in Google Ads and GA4 for:

  1. Calls from ads (call assets) longer than 60 seconds
  2. Clicks on the WhatsApp button
  3. Form submissions
  4. Calls from the website (website call conversion with a forwarding number, where available)

Then import offline outcomes (survey booked, installed) if your CRM allows it. Bidding on real outcomes is what separates profitable solar accounts from expensive ones.

Landing pages decide your cost per click

Google rewards relevance. If the keyword is "3kW solar system price", the page should show 3 kW pricing and subsidy, not a generic home page. Better relevance improves Quality Score, and that usually lowers what you pay per click.

Every solar landing page should have:

  • The subsidy and sizing in the first screen
  • A WhatsApp button and a click-to-call button
  • Real installation photos and Google reviews
  • A short FAQ (net metering, timeline, warranty, loans)
  • Fast loading on mobile

Budget and bidding

Start with Maximise conversions once tracking works, and move to Target CPA after you have steady conversions. Set budgets per city based on how many surveys your team can actually handle; more leads than you can call is wasted money.

Weekly routine

  • Read the search terms report and add negatives
  • Pause ads with low click-through rates
  • Check which ad groups bring calls vs forms
  • Compare cost per lead with cost per survey booked

Google Search works best alongside Meta Ads: Google catches active searchers, Meta creates new demand. See Meta Ads for solar companies and our Google Ads services.

Frequently asked questions

Which keywords bring the best solar leads?

Local and price-intent keywords such as 'solar installer near me', 'solar company in [city]' and '3kW solar system price' usually bring the most ready buyers. Generic terms like 'solar energy' bring researchers and students.

Should solar companies use Performance Max?

Only after Search is profitable and tracking is reliable. PMax needs good conversion data, otherwise it spends on cheap, low-quality placements.

How do I stop paying for 'free solar' searches?

Add negatives such as free, free panel, yojana form, job, salary, franchise, DIY and wholesale, and review the search terms report every week.
