Why education marketing often underperforms

Enquiries only in season

Most institutes get a rush in admission months and silence after. We build always-on awareness plus season-specific campaigns so the pipeline doesn't dry up.

Leads that don't visit

Form fills that never come for a demo class. We qualify by class, course and location, and send demo-class reminders on WhatsApp.

No proof online

Results, faculty and student testimonials sit in WhatsApp groups instead of on your website and Instagram. We turn them into content that builds trust.

Competing with big brands

National edtech brands outspend local institutes. Local targeting, Hindi creatives and a strong Google Business Profile let you win your area.

When should an institute start admission campaigns?+

Start building awareness and retargeting audiences 6 to 8 weeks before your admission window, then increase budget when parents and students are actively deciding. Always-on campaigns at a lower budget keep enquiries coming in the off-season.

Which works better for coaching institutes, Meta or Google Ads?+

Google Search captures students already searching for a course in your area. Meta and YouTube build awareness with results, faculty and student-story videos. Most institutes do best with both, plus a strong Google Business Profile.

Can you run ads for schools?+

Yes. School admission campaigns focus on parents within a realistic travel radius, with campus videos, facilities and a clear visit or admission-form CTA.

Can you make reels for our institute?+

Yes. We edit faculty clips, topper interviews, classroom moments and quick concept explainers into reels, plus AI avatar videos for regular updates.
